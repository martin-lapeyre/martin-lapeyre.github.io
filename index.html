04 — Devices

Devices Developed

Key semiconductor devices designed, fabricated, and characterized during the PhD.

DEV — 001
MIS-HEMT GaN/Al₂O₃ — 650 V class
Metal-insulator-semiconductor HEMT with ALD Al₂O₃ gate dielectric. Achieved 750 V breakdown, 3.2 mS/mm transconductance, f_T = 28 GHz on 2-inch GaN-on-SiC wafers.
Power Electronics ALD Breakdown 750 V
DEV — 002
Gate-Recessed E-mode GaN HEMT
Normally-off (enhancement-mode) transistor using fluorine plasma treatment and recessed gate. Positive V_th = +0.9 V with 200 mA/mm I_DSS. Critical for power converter integration.
E-mode Normally-off Fluorine implant
DEV — 003
Multi-finger RF HEMT — W-band
High-frequency GaN HEMT with 100 nm T-gate for operation above 90 GHz. Gate width 4×25 µm. f_T / f_max = 110 / 195 GHz. Fabricated in collaboration with Thales Research & Technology.
RF / mmWave W-band f_max 195 GHz
